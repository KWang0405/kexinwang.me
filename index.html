Hi! I’m Kexin Wang, a Ph.D. student in Civil Engineering at the University of Southern California, advised by Prof. Ruolin Li.

I study how intelligent agents can make better decisions together, especially when they share the road. My research combines game theory, multi-agent learning, and foundation models to connect individual decisions with system-level goals. My current work explores coordination in mixed-autonomy traffic, cooperation between different autonomous driving models, and how vehicle-level decisions collectively shape transportation systems.

Currently thinking about: mixed-autonomy coordination heterogeneous driving models multi-agent learning local decisions ↔ system dynamics
